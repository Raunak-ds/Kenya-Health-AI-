# Demo script
1. Open the app on a phone or laptop.
2. Show `/health` and confirm `model_ready: true`.
3. Upload a sharp supported-crop leaf photo.
4. Point out quality gate, disease, calibrated confidence, severity disclaimer, and Grad-CAM.
5. Show management/prevention/monitoring with clickable sources.
6. Upload a deliberately blurry/dark image to show rejection.
7. Show an uncertain/OOD example to demonstrate the system does not force a disease prediction.
8. Open `artifacts/test_metrics.json` and confusion matrix for measured results.
