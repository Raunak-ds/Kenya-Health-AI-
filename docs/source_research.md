# Source research record

Accessed during project assembly on 2026-10-02.

## Datasets
- PlantVillage curated re-release: https://huggingface.co/datasets/geraldmc/plantvillage-full — dataset card reports 54,304 images, 38 classes, 14 hosts, CC0-1.0, and leaf-group metadata/split provenance.
- PlantDoc standardized repository: https://github.com/ai-agriculture-circuits-and-systems/plant_doc_detection — reports 2,585 real images, 13 species, 29 disease classes, CC BY 4.0, and points to the original dataset.
