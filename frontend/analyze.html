For a good photo

  • One leaf, filling most of the frame
  • Daylight, no strong shadow
  • Hold steady and tap to focus
  • Include the spots or damage

Avoid

  • Blurry or dark photos
  • Whole fields or many plants
  • Wet leaves with glare
  • Filters or edited images
